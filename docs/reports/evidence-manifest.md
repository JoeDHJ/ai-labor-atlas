# Selected portfolio evidence

This directory is an independent public-data research portfolio, not a journal
submission or a record of client work. Its two cases are the evidence selected by
the authorized project brief. Claims are confined to the actual covered samples.

| Evidence | Reproducible source | Selected output and claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | `intelligence.exposure_case`; local `occupations.csv`; unique SOCs | `exposure_quartiles.csv`, Figure I, brief Table I: exposure quartile wage/employment distribution; 691 complete SOCs |
| E2 | `intelligence.market_comparison`; six public monthly CSVs | `industry_changes.csv`, Figure II, brief Table II: same-period openings, employment and nominal pay comparisons |
| E3 | `staffing.parse_matrix`, `skill_composition`; four 2025 staffing matrices and O*NET 30.3 | `skill_composition.csv`, Figure III, brief Table III: employment-weighted importance and explicitly thresholded skill co-occurrence with coverage |
| E4 | `scenario.run_scenario` and `sensitivity` | Conditional default scenario in report; parameters and trajectory generated by CLI/dashboard, no observed employment response claim |

`selected-evidence.json` contains the selected case aggregates, monthly public
observations, skills, co-occurrence and measurement metadata. It excludes all
occupation-level AIOE rows. `tools/build_portfolio_evidence.py` regenerates the
brief, three CSV tables, that JSON and three PNG/SVG figure pairs from the actual
public-data research run. The report text is maintained alongside these outputs.

Local `data/raw/`, `data/processed/` and `data/research/` retain the source and full
analysis files outside this public evidence directory. No file digest is needed
for provenance; source versions, dates, paths, sizes, schemas and counts are used.
